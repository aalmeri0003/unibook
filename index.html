Welcome To UniBook

The Idea:

Students of KAU need course books in a place that is easy to find and with a fair price. UniBook solves this problem by putting books of KAU faculties in one place to prevent confusion.

Why UniBook:

What makes UniBook special is that you can have the option for both new books or used ones from previous students which at a cheaper price. It focuses on specific segment which is KAU students, so, books do not get mixed with other content.
